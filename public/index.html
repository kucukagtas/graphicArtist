Working Process

From the initial spark of an idea to tactile ink on paper, every project follows an intentional, collaborative creative journey.

1

Concept & Research

Immersion into the project vision, audience exploration, moodboarding, and thematic visual research.

2

Sketch & Vectorize

Iterative thumbnail drafting, typographic exploration, and transforming raw drawings into crisp vector paths.

3

Color & Proofing

Harmonizing curated palettes, testing print textures, halftone angles, and meticulous quality proofing.

4

Print & Delivery

Final prepress production, screenprint pulling, and delivering print-ready files and collectible editions.
